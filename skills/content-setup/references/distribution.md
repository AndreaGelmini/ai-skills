# Distribution

Capture publishing intentions, not assumptions about platform algorithms. Keep preferences separate from current technical requirements.

| Field / document | Purpose / sufficient when |
|---|---|
| `platform` (strategy) | At least one intended destination, with account distinction where relevant. One document per destination/account: goal, audience, preferred formats/media and language or style overrides where known. Distinguish selected versus potential destinations in the body; no login/credentials or authorization to publish. |
| `language`, `timezone` (technical) | Default writing language and scheduling timezone. Ask only missing details; use named timezone when known. Keep per-platform language overrides in that platform document only. |
| `cadence` (strategy) | General sustainable rhythm, fixed or flexible. Put rubric-specific or platform-specific cadence in its own document instead of duplicating it here. |

Only an intended destination is structurally required. Other choices may be declined or deferred. Do not invent a publishing schedule or current platform limitations; verify technical constraints when preparing content.
