---
name: restate-my-goals
description: >-
  Restates the user's goals and the problem they want to solve. Use this skill when the user
  asks you to put their goals into your own words, check your understanding of their problem,
  or says "Restate in your own words what you think my goals are and what the problem I'm trying to solve is."
  Recognize equivalent requests in the conversation's language. Do not use for simple summaries
  or requests to perform a task without first checking understanding.
---

Use the relevant conversation, giving the user's latest corrections priority. Respond in a few natural sentences in the language of the conversation. Start with the desired outcome, for example, “From what I understand, you want to…” Then explain the main difficulty the user is trying to overcome.

Attribute only explicit statements to the user. Mark inferences with phrases such as “It sounds like…” and ground them in a detail from the conversation when helpful. Mention uncertainty only when it changes how you understand the goal or problem. Leave unsupported details open.

Offer solutions, plans, or suggestions only when asked.
