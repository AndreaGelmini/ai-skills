---
name: session-status
description: >-
  Produces a whole-session status and handoff briefing covering context, needs, decisions,
  and progress. Use this skill when the user asks to take stock, get the current state of
  a conversation or its work, check progress against an existing goal or plan, or prepare
  a session compact or handoff, including equivalent requests in any language such as
  "punto della situazione". Do not use for simple summaries, goal restatement alone,
  or direct task execution without a request to review understanding or status.
---

Review the entire available session from its starting context to its latest state, including corrections, pauses, topic changes, and accessible forks or branches. Use available conversation retrieval when needed and permitted. Explain any gaps in coverage. Treat a branch mentioned but unavailable as unverified, and request its context when material. Link branches only with verified references. Preserve divergent outcomes and attribute them to their branches until the user resolves them.

Respond in the conversation's language, concisely but with enough detail to resume the work. Open naturally, for example, “From what I understand, here is where things stand…” Use these sections, translating their headings:

- **Starting context** — The initial situation, goals, and constraints.
- **Current context** — Where the session has arrived and a short explanation of the changes, findings, and branches that led here.
- **Current needs** — What the user needs now. Include newly emerged needs when present and distinguish them from the original ones.
- **Decisions** — When relevant, identify decisions made, still pending, and superseded. Preserve who made or confirmed each decision when that affects its authority.
- **Progress** — For work with an established goal, process, or plan, map the existing milestones to completed, in progress, blocked, or pending, using evidence from the session. Separate attempted actions from verified outcomes. Include relevant artifacts and verified links. State when progress cannot be established.

Distinguish explicit user statements and confirmed results from your interpretations. Mark deductions as tentative. Ask short, targeted questions about material doubts, conflicting instructions, or missing context, while providing the supported portion of the briefing. Avoid inventing decisions, branch links, completion claims, or percentages.

Offer solutions, new plans, or suggestions only when requested. Describe existing next steps only when already agreed in the session.
